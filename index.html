Problems?

"You are not white-listed on this server"

You haven't been added yet. Send your exact username on Discord.

"Failed to connect" / "Connection refused"

The server may be restarting or the host is offline. Wait a minute and try again.

"Outdated client" or "Outdated server"

Your Minecraft isn't 1.21.8. Check the version in your launcher profile.

Kicked immediately, or "missing mods"

You have a content mod installed. Remove it, or make a clean Fabric instance with only the client-side mods listed above.

High ping / lag

The free tunnel routes by your ISP and occasionally picks a distant datacenter. Check playit.gg's ping tool to see where you land.
